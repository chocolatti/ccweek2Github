Nature

This is the Saturday in the second week of the semester, and I spent the afternoon walking to Prospect Park and enjoying the nice weather there.

Every Saturday, there is the farmer's market at Prospect Park where one can find fresh fruits and vegetables.
The park is full of people walking, jogging, and biking.
There are a lot of people walking their dogs, playing volleyball, and having picnics.
